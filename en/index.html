About the practice

Heart care is a long-term
partnership

Jae Hyung Cho, MD Cardiology provides high-quality cardiology care to adults with a wide range of heart conditions — from testing and diagnosis to long-term follow-up, in one place.

Meet Dr. Cho
01

Extensive clinical experience

More than 15 years of experience, including a cardiology fellowship at Cedars-Sinai and a Clinical Assistant Professor role at Cleveland Clinic.

02

Comprehensive heart testing

Echocardiography, exercise stress testing, vascular ultrasound, and other tests for an accurate diagnosis.

03

Specialized arrhythmia care

A physician with a PhD in arrhythmia research cares for palpitations, atrial fibrillation, and other heart-rhythm problems.

04

Clear explanations

We explain your diagnosis and options in plain language and coordinate closely with your primary care physician.
