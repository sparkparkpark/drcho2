Website Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026

This policy explains what information this website collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. It applies to this website only. Information you share with us as a patient is governed by our Notice of Privacy Practices.

Information We Collect

  • This website does not ask you to enter medical information, and does not store any protected health information. Please do not send medical information through this website or by email.
  • Server logs: like most websites, our hosting provider automatically records limited technical data (IP address, browser type, pages visited, time of visit) for security and reliability. Logs are retained for a limited period.
  • Third-party resources: pages on this site load a web font (Pretendard) from a third-party content delivery network (jsDelivr). That provider receives your IP address and browser information when delivering the file. We do not use analytics.
  • Embedded map: the Contact page shows a Google Maps map. When that page loads, Google receives your IP address and browser information and may set cookies; Google's privacy policy applies to the map.
  • Information you send us: if you call, email, or submit a non-clinical inquiry, we receive what you choose to share (for example, your name and phone number) to respond to you.

Cookies and Tracking

This website does not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, session-replay tools, or third-party chat widgets. We do not use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or similar advertising technologies. Because we do not track you across sites, we treat Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals as already honored.

Videos: educational videos are hosted on YouTube. This site shows preview images stored on our own server and links to YouTube; nothing is loaded from YouTube unless you click a video, which opens YouTube, where Google's privacy policy applies.

How We Use and Share Information

  • To operate, secure, and improve this website and to respond to your inquiries.
  • Service providers: our website host processes technical data on our behalf under contracts that restrict their use of it.
  • We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Your California Privacy Rights

California residents may have the right to know what personal information we collect, to request deletion or correction, and to not be discriminated against for exercising these rights. Medical information covered by HIPAA or the CMIA is handled under those laws instead. To make a request, contact us using the details below. We will verify your identity before responding.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page with an updated effective date.
