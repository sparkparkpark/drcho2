Notice of Privacy Practices

Effective October 1, 2026

THIS NOTICE DESCRIBES HOW MEDICAL INFORMATION ABOUT YOU MAY BE USED AND DISCLOSED AND HOW YOU CAN GET ACCESS TO THIS INFORMATION. PLEASE REVIEW IT CAREFULLY.

Our Commitment

We are required by law to maintain the privacy of your protected health information (PHI), to give you this notice of our legal duties and privacy practices, and to follow the terms of the notice currently in effect. California law (the Confidentiality of Medical Information Act) provides additional protections, and where it is more protective than federal law we follow California law.

How We May Use and Disclose Your Health Information

  • Treatment: to provide, coordinate, or manage your care, including sharing with other providers, hospitals, and laboratories involved in your care.
  • Payment: to bill and collect payment from you, your health plan, or other payers.
  • Health care operations: quality assessment, training, licensing, accreditation, and business management.
  • Appointment reminders, treatment alternatives, and health-related services.
  • As required by law, for public health activities, for health oversight, in judicial and administrative proceedings, to law enforcement as permitted, to avert a serious threat to health or safety, and for workers' compensation as permitted by law.

Uses and Disclosures That Require Your Written Authorization

We will not use or disclose your PHI for marketing, sell your PHI, disclose psychotherapy notes, or post your name, photograph, or story as a testimonial on our website or social media without your written authorization. You may revoke an authorization in writing at any time, except to the extent we have already acted on it.

Your Rights

  • Inspect and obtain a copy of your medical record (under California law, inspection within 5 working days and copies within 15 days of a written request; a reasonable fee may apply).
  • Request an amendment of your record.
  • Receive an accounting of certain disclosures.
  • Request restrictions on uses and disclosures. If you pay in full out of pocket for a service, you may request that we not disclose it to your health plan and we must agree.
  • Request confidential communications by alternative means or at alternative locations.
  • Receive a paper copy of this notice on request, even if you agreed to receive it electronically.
  • Be notified following a breach of unsecured PHI.

Complaints

If you believe your privacy rights have been violated, you may file a complaint with our Privacy Officer (contact information below) or with the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services, Office for Civil Rights, 200 Independence Avenue S.W., Washington, D.C. 20201, 1-800-368-1019, www.hhs.gov/ocr/privacy/hipaa/complaints. We will not retaliate against you for filing a complaint.

Changes to This Notice

We reserve the right to change this notice and to make the revised notice effective for PHI we already have. The current notice is posted in our office and on this website, and is available on request.
